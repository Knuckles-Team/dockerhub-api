| `CONN-DOCKERHUB-API-CONNECTOR-CONTENT-R003` | **Destructive operations use the SDK's one declaration and guard.** The 14 operations that delete or remove tokens, groups, members and invites, change organization settings, or write to the registry (`delete_access_token`, `delete_org_access_token`, `delete_group`, `remove_group_member`, `remove_org_member`, `delete_invite`, `update_org_settings`, `put_manifest`, `delete_manifest`, `delete_blob`, `start_upload`, `upload_chunk`, `complete_upload`, `mount_blob`) carry the SDK declaration `destructive_operation` (`SCION-CONNECTOR-CONTROL-R930`, provider). Shape: `@destructive_operation` on the 14 `Api` methods; the client-level gate (`_guard_destructive`, `DestructiveOperationError`, the `allow_destructive` constructor option) is deleted.
